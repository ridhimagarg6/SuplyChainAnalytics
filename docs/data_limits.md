**Data limits**

These points are important when explaining the results.

- All order dates checked were 26 May 2013. The current data cannot show changes across months or years.
- A row count in `order_list` counts records. It should not automatically be called a count of unique shipments.
- A plant's share of order quantity is not the same as the amount of its daily capacity that was used.
- The customer group limits were chosen for this analysis. They are not official business rules.
- Delivery rates should be compared using the same route, transport type, service and weight range.
- The high delivery rate found in the route check may need investigation. It is not proof of an error.
- Check that table joins do not create extra repeated rows before using joined totals.

These limits should also be shown in any future report or dashboard.
