**Customer findings**

**What we wanted to know**

Which customers ordered the most quantity, and how much of the total did they represent?

**What the results showed**

- The data had 46 different customers.
- Customer `V55555555_5` had 8,020,417 units.
- This customer represented 27.18% of the recorded quantity.
- The five largest customer shares were 27.18%, 17.94%, 6.49%, 6.47% and 5.12%.
- The nine largest customers together represented 80.18% of the total quantity.

**What this means**

A large part of the recorded quantity came from a small group of customers. The largest customer had the biggest share.

**What this does not tell us**

These results describe the data we have. They do not prove that the business depends on these customers or show what customers will order in the future. The customer groups in the SQL use sample limits chosen for practice.
